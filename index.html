Diagnostic Insight

Trace Elements (HTMA) vs. Standard Blood Tests

Why test tissue instead of blood? A standard blood test provides an instantaneous snapshot of circulating minerals that your body tightly regulates day-to-day[cite: 1]. However, blood levels can appear "normal" even when cellular tissues are severely depleted[cite: 1].

HTMA acts like a 3-month video recording instead of a single photo[cite: 1]. As hair grows, it retains a timeline of stored tissue minerals and toxic heavy metals[cite: 1]. This makes HTMA superior for catching chronic stress patterns, slow-developing mineral deficiencies, and heavy metal accumulation that blood draws miss completely[cite: 1].

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the pre-consultation intake required?

By filling out the intake prior to booking, your provider can review your medical history, symptoms, and referral/lab interests beforehand. This ensures 100% of your call time is spent giving you actionable solutions and guidance.

Can I order labs or protocols à la carte without a consultation?

Yes! You can order targeted preset reset protocols and practitioner-grade supplements directly through our Fullscript dispensary, or request functional lab ordering through our portal without needing to complete an initial long-form consultation[cite: 1].

What is medical coaching, support, and advocacy?

Many patients feel overwhelmed during doctor visits. We help you review complex lab results, understand diagnosis details, evaluate potential medication side effects, and draft structured questions so you can advocate effectively when speaking with your PCP or specialists.

How does the direct referral to the sister business work?

If you are seeking medical weight loss (e.g., GLP-1s) or HRT, we order your necessary readiness labs. Once complete, you receive a direct referral to our clinical partner business to begin medication immediately—without having to pay for a second intake or repeat testing.

How do the preset reset protocols work?

We offer targeted, pre-structured protocols (Gastrointestinal, Fasting/Detox, Metabolic, Cellular Health, and Nervous System). You can pair them with our $200/mo package for weekly 30-minute coaching check-ins as you navigate your regimen.
