Diagnostic Insight

Trace Elements (HTMA) vs. Standard Blood Tests

Why test tissue instead of blood? A standard blood test provides an instantaneous snapshot of circulating minerals that your body tightly regulates day-to-day[cite: 1]. However, blood levels can appear "normal" even when cellular tissues are severely depleted[cite: 1].

HTMA acts like a 3-month video recording instead of a single photo[cite: 1]. As hair grows, it retains a timeline of stored tissue minerals and toxic heavy metals[cite: 1]. This makes HTMA superior for catching chronic stress patterns, slow-developing mineral deficiencies, and heavy metal accumulation that blood draws miss completely[cite: 1].

Do I need to see a doctor before booking a coaching session?

No. You can book directly with us at any time. We focus on root-cause coaching, lifestyle protocols, and helping you navigate your options clearly before or alongside traditional healthcare.

How does the Lip Bunny Rx prescription referral work?

If you're interested in GLP-1 weight management or HRT, we complete your initial readiness assessment and baseline lab ordering during your Wellness Hunny session. We then transfer your file directly to Lip Bunny Rx so you can start therapy with no duplicate intake forms.

Can I order lab panels without scheduling a consultation?

Yes. All functional lab panels (HTMA, Stool, Blood work) are available à la carte directly through our portal. If you'd like expert guidance on interpreting your results, you can add a Lab Interpretation Add-On at any time.
