No. You can book directly with us at any time. We focus on root-cause coaching, lifestyle protocols, and helping you navigate your options clearly before or alongside traditional healthcare.

If you're interested in GLP-1 weight management or HRT, we complete your initial readiness assessment and baseline lab ordering during your Wellness Hunny session. We then transfer your file directly to Lip Bunny Rx so you can start therapy with no duplicate intake forms.
