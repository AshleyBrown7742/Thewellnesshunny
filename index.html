Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the pre-consultation intake required?

By filling out the intake prior to booking, your provider can review your medical history, symptoms, and referral/lab interests beforehand. This ensures 100% of your call time is spent giving you actionable solutions and guidance.

How does the direct referral to the sister business work?

If you are seeking medical weight loss (e.g., GLP-1s) or HRT, we order your necessary readiness labs. Once complete, you receive a direct referral to our clinical partner business to begin medication immediately—without having to pay for a second intake or repeat testing.

What is medical advocacy help?

Many patients feel overwhelmed during doctor visits. We help you review complex lab results, understand diagnosis details, evaluate potential medication side effects, and draft structured questions so you can advocate effectively when speaking with your PCP or specialists.

How do the preset reset protocols work?

We offer targeted, pre-structured protocols (Gastrointestinal, Fasting/Detox, Metabolic, Cellular Health, and Nervous System). You can use your $65 check-in sessions as ongoing coaching appointments while you navigate these protocols.
