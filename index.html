Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Why start with a detox instead of medication?

Medication works best on a balanced foundation. The Bunny Reset shifts your baseline first. Some patients find they need less medication, or none at all. Others start GLP-1 or HRT with fewer side effects.

Why test the gut?

Research continues to link gut health to hormones, inflammation, mood, and metabolism. Testing at the start and end shows exactly how your gut responded to the program.

Can I start with HRT or GLP-1?

Every journey starts with the Bunny Reset. There is no shortcut door, because medication works best on a balanced foundation. If perimenopause or menopause symptoms are affecting your quality of life, bioidentical HRT may be started during your Reset after your baseline labs. GLP-1, including microdosing, is available from week 8, once your follow-up labs show your body is ready.

Are visits in person or virtual?

Program visits and blood draws are in person at Lip Bunny Beauty in Fall River, MA. Some follow-up visits can be done virtually.

Is the program open to men?

Yes. The Bunny Reset, Hoptimization, GLP-1, testing, injectables, and IV therapy are all available to men. Bioidentical HRT is offered for women only.

How do I pay for the program?

The Bunny Reset is $2,500, the same price for every patient. Financing is available through Cherry and CareCredit, so you can spread payments over time.
