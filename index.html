Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Why start with a detox instead of medication?

Medication works best on a balanced foundation. The Detox Primer shifts your baseline first. Some patients find they need less medication, or none at all. Others start GLP-1 or HRT with fewer side effects.

Why test the gut?

Research continues to link gut health to hormones, inflammation, mood, and metabolism. Testing at the start and end shows exactly how your gut responded to the program.

Can I start GLP-1 or HRT right away?

Every patient completes the Detox Primer first. After graduation, your WellPro reviews your retest labs and recommends GLP-1, HRT, both, or detox support alone.

Are visits in person or virtual?

Program visits and blood draws are in person at Lip Bunny Beauty in Fall River, MA. Some follow-up visits can be done virtually.

Is the program open to men?

Yes. The Detox Primer is designed for both women and men.

How do I pay for the program?

The Detox Primer is $2,500, the same price for every patient. Financing is available through Cherry and CareCredit, so you can spread payments over time.
