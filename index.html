Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How is root-cause coaching different from traditional care?

Conventional medicine excels at managing acute symptoms and diagnosing disease. Root-cause coaching investigates *why* those symptoms are happening in the first place—focusing on gut integrity, stress load, and metabolic health to address issues at their origin through lifestyle, nutritional, and supplement support.

Is everything conducted virtually?

Yes. All 1:1 strategy calls and continuous support are delivered through a secure virtual client portal, so you can transform your health from anywhere.

Do I need lab tests to start?

Not at all. We gather immense insight from your detailed health timeline, symptom patterns, and daily lifestyle markers. While specialized functional lab work may be recommended down the line to get deeper insights, it is never required to begin your healing journey. If you already have recent lab work from a doctor, we'll happily review it together during your intake.

How quickly will I see progress?

Most clients notice meaningful shifts in energy, sleep quality, and digestion within the first 3 to 4 weeks, with deep, lasting physical transformation consolidating over 3 to 6 months.
