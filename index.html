Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to sign up for a monthly contract?

No! You can book individual 1:1 check-in sessions at $65 whenever you need them. Monthly bundles are completely optional for clients who want bi-weekly check-ins and discounted per-session pricing.

How is root-cause coaching different from traditional care?

Conventional medicine excels at managing acute symptoms and diagnosing disease. Root-cause coaching investigates *why* those symptoms are happening in the first place—focusing on gut integrity, stress load, and metabolic health to address issues at their origin through lifestyle, nutritional, and supplement support.

Is everything conducted virtually?

Yes. All strategy calls and ongoing support are delivered through a secure virtual client portal, so you can work on your health from anywhere.

Do I need lab tests to start?

Not at all. We gather immense insight from your detailed health timeline, symptom patterns, and daily lifestyle markers. While specialized functional lab work (like gut health testing or mineral panels) may be recommended down the line, it is never required to begin your healing journey.
