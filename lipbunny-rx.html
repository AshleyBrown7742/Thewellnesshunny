Safety Comes First

Labs Before and During Treatment

No prescription starts without labs. Every Lip Bunny Rx patient gets the full Bunny Baseline at the start and once a year after that. Between those, follow-up panels keep your treatment safe and your dose right. All draws are done in person at Lip Bunny Beauty in Fall River.

The Bunny Baseline At the Start + Every Year
One deep, whole-body panel for every patient. Drawn before your Reset begins, and repeated every year while you are on Lip Bunny Rx. Your first panel is included in the Bunny Reset.
  • Metabolic + Cardiovascular: CMP, A1c, fasting insulin, lipid panel, ApoB, hsCRP, homocysteine
  • Liver + Pancreas: GGT, amylase, lipase
  • Thyroid: TSH, Free T3, Free T4, TPO antibodies
  • Sex Hormones: Estradiol, progesterone, FSH, free and total testosterone with SHBG
  • Blood, Vitamins + Minerals: CBC, iron, ferritin, B12, folate, vitamin D, RBC zinc, copper, and magnesium
  • Heavy Metals: Arsenic, lead, mercury
GLP-1 Follow-Up Panel
Checks your metabolic, liver, pancreas, and thyroid markers while on GLP-1, so we can adjust your dose safely.
  • CBC, CMP, GGT, amylase, lipase
  • TSH, Free T3
  • Fasting insulin, A1c, lipid panel
Schedule set by your provider based on your dose and progress.
HRT Follow-Up Panel
Tracks your hormone levels and thyroid while on HRT, so your dose matches how you feel and what your body needs.
  • Estradiol, progesterone, FSH
  • Free and total testosterone with SHBG
  • TSH, Free T3
Schedule set by your provider based on your dose and progress.
